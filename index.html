    How it works

    Patterns cost less

    Plain math gives P@ssw0rd2024! about 85 bits. Attackers try common words, leetspeak, years and keyboard runs first, so each pattern here is charged only what it really costs to guess. That one drops to about 22 bits.

    Length wins

    Every random character multiplies the work. Four or five random words beat a short password full of symbols, and they're far easier to remember.

    Nothing is sent

    No fetch(), no APIs, no analytics and no libraries. The fonts are stored with the page. The counter in the black bar shows every request the page makes, so you don't have to take my word for it.
